# Travel Plan — Lisbon

Lisbon is a coastal city built on seven hills. The best time to visit is May to September.

## Day 1 — Alfama

Wander the Alfama district. Stop at Miradouro de Santa Luzia for the view over the Tagus river. Eat grilled sardines at a tasca.

## Day 2 — Belém

Take the tram to Belém. Visit the Jerónimos Monastery and the Belém Tower. Eat a pastel de nata at the original Pastéis de Belém café.

## Day 3 — Sintra day trip

Take the train from Rossio station to Sintra (40 minutes). Visit the Pena Palace and walk the gardens of Quinta da Regaleira.
